Esa es la clave con estudiantes A1-A2 que _sí tienen base pero no fluidez aún_: no repetir lo ultra básico, sino **usar estructuras conocidas para hablar con más intención, conexión y espontaneidad**.

➡️ _Introducirse hablando de gustos y preferencias_, y de paso integrar:

- **Verbos de preferencia + -ing** (_like dancing_, _enjoy listening to music…_)
    
- **Conectores básicos** (_because, but, and_)
    
- **Adjetivos de opinión** (_fun, boring, relaxing…_)
    
- **Vocabulario más personal / nuevo, no solo “name-age-country”***
    

Esto te permite que la clase sea una **presentación personal más rica**, sin parecer básica.

### 📘 Grammar Focus – Verbs of Preference + -ing + Connectors

**1️⃣ Verbs of Preference**

- When we talk about things we **like or don’t like**, we use:  
    **like/ love / hate / enjoy / prefer/ I don’t mind+ verb + -ing**
    
- Examples:
    
    - I **like reading**.
        
    - She **loves dancing**.
        
    - We **hate cooking**.
        

💡 Tip: You can say “I like to read,” but we focus on **-ing** to talk naturally about activities.

**2️⃣ Connectors: because, and, but**

- To **give reasons** or **connect ideas**, we use:
    
    - **because** → to explain why
        
        - “I like swimming **because it’s relaxing**.”
            
    - **and** → to add information
        
        - “I like hiking **because it’s healthy and adventurous**.”
            
    - **but** → to show contrast
        
        - “I like reading, **but I don’t like watching TV**.”
            

**3️⃣ Examples combining everything**

- “I like hiking **because it’s adventurous and healthy**.”
    
- “She loves dancing, **but she hates swimming**.”
    
- “We enjoy snorkeling **because it’s exciting and fun**.”
    

⚠️ **Common mistakes:**  
❌ “I like to watch movies.” → correct but we want to focus on **-ing**  
✅ “I like **watching movies**.” → correct and more natural
